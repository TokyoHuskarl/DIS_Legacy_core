 Several years after the dragonfolks began their colonization of Doradora^Island. Having repelled repeated raids, the dragonfolk at last secured ^an official ceasefire agreement with the King of the Northlanders.^ During the negotiations, the Northlander King revealed to the Empyrean ^Lord that the Empire had requested an attack against the dragonfolk.^ Thus were sown the seeds of suspicion and resentment toward the Empire!^Among the hot-blooded dragonkind, reckless voices cried out for ^retribution, while the bloodthirsty Northlanders shamelessly offered their ^support.^ Knowing well the power of the Empire, Empyrean Lord Orthunass was, of ^course, reluctant to confront them. However, reports from dispatched ^exchange students indicated that the Empire was now facing unprecedented ^political instability from within, and its military was currently ^undergoing downsizing and reorganization.^ Even the once-mighty Imperial Legions, which had once dominated the ^continent and even defeated invading outerworld demons, were now struggling ^to maintain their equipment.^ With newfound conviction in both the Empire's weakening and her own ^destiny, Orthunass undertakes a gamble of historic proportions.^ Marching southward into the Imperial province of Niossia, she seeks to ^reclaim the skies of the continent for the dragonfolks!^
