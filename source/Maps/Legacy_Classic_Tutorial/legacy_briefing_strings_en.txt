 A long, long time ago, even before the age of swords and magic, there was a Great Empire that ruled over the continent.^ Under its hegemony, races of all shapes and forms lived in peace, without hatred toward one another.^ Within The Empire, as long as one held citizenship, ^they were considered equal, regardless of their species, as a fellow "Civis".
 ^Among them, the Dragonfolk were a nomadic race that had wandered the skies since ancient times.^ Over time, three major clans began to distinguish themselves: ^the intelligent Nasun, the dexterous Draco, and the mighty Drana.^ The Dragonfolk thrived through a life of wandering, ^taking on roles only when necessary and cooperating as needed.
 However, the time for the Dragonfolk to settle had finally arrived.^ The Empire, facing financial difficulties, set its sights on the vast wealth hoarded by the dragons ^and began demanding exorbitant airspace passage taxes.^ To escape The Empire’s watchful eye, they prepared to establish a new home on a small, uninhabited island in the Northern Sea.
 Of course, such a settlement required strong leadership.^ Thus, the dragons entrusted absolute authority to Orthunass, ^a female dragon of the Nasun clan who had once studied abroad in The Empire.
 Now, the fate of the Dragonfolk rests upon her two wings.^ Will they and their civilization withstand the test of time?
